Assumptions and caveats
  • This is a scenario model built from public fundamentals (2024 presidential result, generic-ballot polling, fundraising reports, race ratings), not a poll of this specific race — treat the probabilities as illustrative, not predictive.
  • District lean defaults to R+8.5: Trump carried NC-11 by about 10 points in 2024 against a national popular-vote win of roughly 1.5 points.
  • National environment defaults to the September 2026 generic-ballot average (roughly D+6 to D+7.6 depending on aggregator).
  • Open-seat, fundraising, and local-trend sliders are judgment calls reflecting Chuck Edwards' August 2026 withdrawal after a House Ethics Committee censure recommendation, Jamie Ager's reported cash-on-hand advantage, and the district's decade-long leftward drift plus Hurricane Helene recovery frustration — adjust freely.
  • Comparison figures (Kalshi, Cook Political Report, Sabato's Crystal Ball) are snapshots from late August 2026 and will have moved by the time you read this.
